### Libraries and distributions

The third-party libraries the project uses are declared as dependencies in the pom.xml and
package.json files of the modules, and are not part of this repository. Each distribution (the
standalone jar, the Windows and macOS installers and the Docker image) contains a NOTICE.md
generated during the build, which lists the third-party content of that distribution, together
with the files DEPENDENCIES and sbom.spdx.json (SPDX 2.3) and the license texts in the folder
LICENSES.
